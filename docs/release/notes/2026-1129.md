  states, version-aware stylesheet loading and restored dashboard/proxy actions.

## Preserved runtime contracts

Legacy dataplane writers remain authoritative and frozen. Mode-1 firewall
DNS interception and dnsmasq listen on port 53; dnsmasq upstream and Mihomo
DNS remain separate at 127.0.0.1:7874. Mark 0x162, mask 0xffffffff, table 354
and preference 1888 remain unchanged. Shadow is disabled by default;
CENTRAL_ACTIVE and CENTRAL_NFT_APPLY are not approved.
